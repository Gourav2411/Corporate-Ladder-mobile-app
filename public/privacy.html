3. Why we collect it

  • To save your progress and sync it across devices (legitimate interest / contract performance).
  • To run public/private leaderboards, multiplayer rooms, and Companies HQ (contract performance).
  • To let you publish posts in the Watercooler / Companies / Bounty Board (your explicit action).
  • To send a one-time email-verification link at sign-up (security & double opt-in).
  • If you opt-in to product updates: to email you about new modes, seasons, and Layoff Friday tournaments. This is granular, opt-in only, off by default, revocable in one tap.

We do not use your data for behavioural advertising, profiling, scoring, ML model training, or sale to third parties.

4. Data sharing

We share your data only with the service providers required to run the Game:

  • Google — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Hosting. Stores your account, profile and game data. See Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy.
  • Google Gemini API — only if you use the Roast My Career feature. The four short fields you type are sent directly from your device to Google using your own personal Gemini API key. We never see, log, or store these inputs.

We do not have a CRM, analytics provider, ad network, error-tracking SDK, or any other third-party data processor.

5. Where data is stored

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore data is hosted on Google Cloud, primarily in us-central1 with Google’s standard global redundancy. EU/UK users’ data may transit US infrastructure under Google’s EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Google is a certified participant). All traffic is TLS-encrypted; data at rest is AES-256 encrypted by Google.

6. Retention

  • Account & game data: kept for as long as your account is active. Deleted within seconds when you tap Profile → Delete Account, or within 14 days of a written deletion request.
  • Public posts (Watercooler, Bounties, Companies): retained even after account deletion but anonymised — the authorId is stripped and the displayName is replaced with “[deleted]”. You can request hard-deletion by emailing us.
  • Backups: Firebase keeps point-in-time recovery for up to 7 days. After that interval your deleted data is unrecoverable.
  • Email verification token: invalidated 1 hour after issue.

10. Security

  • All network traffic is TLS 1.2+.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Auth (we never see the plaintext or any hash).
  • Firestore security rules enforce: only the owning user can update their own document; scores are write-only by the owning user; public posts are read-only by everyone but mutable only by the author.
  • R8/ProGuard strips logs in release builds; the keystore is not committed.
  • Network security config disallows cleartext HTTP traffic.
  • We’ll notify affected users within 72 hours of any confirmed personal-data breach, per GDPR Art. 33/34.

11. Account & data deletion

You can permanently erase your account at any time, free of charge:

  1. In-app, instant: open the app → tap your avatar → scroll to Delete Account → confirm. Your Firestore profile and Firebase Auth record are wiped within seconds.
  2. By email, within 14 days: email privacy@corporateladder.game from the address you used to sign in, subject “Delete my data”.

For a granular breakdown of what is hard-deleted vs. anonymised, see /account-deletion.html. To revoke Google sign-in access, visit myaccount.google.com/connections.
