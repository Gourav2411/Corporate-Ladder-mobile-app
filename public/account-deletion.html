Account & Data Deletion · Effective Q1 2026

Delete your Corporate Ladder Simulator account

You can permanently delete your account and personal data from Corporate Ladder Simulator (developer: Gourav K., package app.corporateladder.game) at any time, free of charge, no questions asked.

Fastest method

In-app deletion (1 minute)

  1. Open the app (Android or web).
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Tap your avatar in the top right → scroll to the bottom of the profile sheet.
  4. Tap DELETE_ACCOUNT → review the warning → tap YES, DELETE EVERYTHING.
  5. You'll see your Exit Interview — a one-time satirical farewell card with your career stats and one-tap share buttons. Sharing is completely optional; the deletion is already finished by the time you see it.

If the app prompts you to "sign in again", that's a Firebase security check for sensitive operations — sign out, sign back in with the same Google account, and retry the deletion. The deletion is instant once confirmed.

Email-based deletion

Prefer not to install the app, or already uninstalled it? Email us:

To: privacy@corporateladder.game
Subject: Delete my data
From: the same Google email you used to sign in

We will erase your account within 14 days and confirm by reply. No identity-document upload required.
