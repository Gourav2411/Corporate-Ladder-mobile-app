Privacy Policy · Effective Q1 2026

Corporate Ladder Simulator

This document describes what data Corporate Ladder Simulator (the "Game") collects, why, and how to remove it. The Game is provided by the developer ("we"). The same policy applies to the web version and the Android app (package app.corporateladder.game).

1. What we collect

  • Authentication info (only if you sign in): your Google account email, display name, and Firebase user ID. Provided by Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication.
  • Game data: scores, lifetime synergy, unlocked skills, achievements, daily streak, season wins, and entries you choose to post (Watercooler messages, Bounty board posts, Company memberships).
  • Roast My Career inputs (only if you use that feature): the four text fields you enter (job title, years, buzzword, metric). These are sent to Google Gemini using your personal Gemini API key, which is stored only on your device. We do not receive or store these inputs.

We do not collect: location, contacts, microphone, camera, browsing history, advertising IDs, biometrics, or any sensor data. The app does not show ads and does not contain in-app purchases.

2. Why we collect it

  • To save your progress across devices.
  • To run leaderboards and multiplayer features.
  • To let you publish posts in the Watercooler / Companies / Bounty Board.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Where it lives

  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud, project gen-lang-client-0540931255) store your account and game data, in transit over TLS and at rest with Google-managed encryption.
  • Your device stores your local high score, your skin choice, your Gemini API key (Roast feature only), and a Capacitor preferences cache.

4. Data sharing

We share your data only with the service providers required to run the Game:

  • Google (Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, Gemini API) — see Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy.

5. Children

Corporate Ladder Simulator is satire about adult workplaces. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not sign in or post in public channels.

6. Your rights & data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and personal data at any time, free of charge:

  1. In-app (recommended, instant): Open the app → tap your avatar → scroll to the bottom of the profile sheet → tap DELETE_ACCOUNT → confirm. Your Firestore profile and Firebase Auth record are erased immediately.
  2. By email (within 14 days): email privacy@corporateladder.game from the address you used to sign in, with the subject line "Delete my data". We will erase your Firestore record and revoke your Authentication record within 14 days and reply to confirm.

For a full breakdown of what gets deleted, what is anonymized, and what is retained, see our dedicated page: /account-deletion.html.

You can also revoke the app's Google sign-in access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

7. Security

All network traffic uses TLS. Firestore security rules enforce that only you can update your own user document, and that scores are only writable by the owning user. We do not log raw passwords (Google handles auth).

8. Changes to this policy

If material changes are made, we will update this page and bump the effective date at the top.
